# The Nightmare on Consulting Work Culture

People are come and go in consulting, so does for projects availability bacause it really dependent on the sales pipeline performance. Getting a chargeable project is the most important KPI for almost every consultant especially for non managerial positions. The higher position we at, finding a new project might be challenging as our headcost is high compared to newly joined analyst or associates.

	Unspoken rule in any consulting: ABC - always be chargeable and ABN - always be networking

In most of the cases, ABC (always be charged) condition can only be achieved with a sustain ABN(always be networking). Network wont be established in weeks or month. It formed over the time and must be actively maintained by continuous bonding through various project/business development or outing. a Solid network might fallen if our relationship with leadership is not getting taken care of.

No project means no chargeable work. Such condition is commonly called as "on bench" in consulting industry. The benchtime will vary depending on our existing network and market demand. Surviving bench is hard even for someone with good industry experiences but without any market demands and weak internal network in the firms. 

## Nearmiss: Swollen in Busy Delivery and Miss to Networking 

Even for senior consultant, networking can be tricky especially if their current project is very demanding and require extra attention which leaving no time and room to network outside the project. Such condition is very dangerous for our longetivity on the firm. The project manager of current project might or may not plan for our next assignment. 

All depend on the project roster. If both of the PM and us are came from same practices, most likely the next project assingment will be secured. However, its not rare that the PM actually came from different practice (cross function project).

	&mdash; Are we cooked ?

Its all depend on the remaining runway before the roll off is triggered. Generally, 2 months of runway should be the bare minimum. Less than that it could be hard to land a new assignment after the project is ended. Ideally the networking activity should be done continuously without a fixed grace period before the official roll off date. 

# Networking Plan for Two Months Runway to Land a Role

Realisticly, two months runway is pretty short unless there are several sales pipeline that about to materilize in such timeframe. 

	Its better late than never, lets plan it

Suppose the roll off date is on end of october (now is end of august), so we have arround 2 months runway. There will be three part of the networking plan

1. **Internal Networking**: find an opportunity/leads within our internal practices from our past project and its also common to have network outside from our practice even on crossborder scenario especially at big consulting firm.
2. **Follow up & Conversion**: the grinding phase. require strong GRIT and good communication strategy. Rule of thumb: **do not beg for project**, it wont be appealing for the project owner. Better to frame the communication as "**achievement and skill pitching**". "I have done {XXX}, i think i could bring {XXX} value to your practice/current project/next opportunity." 
3. **The contigency**: All risk must be mitigated which should also cover the workcases: unemployment scenario.

Internal networking will be the main activities that need to executed immediately. At this stage, do not rush into asking whether there is a role opening on their project as it will only devalue ourself. Only ask it once we got the leads information and current sales pipeline landscape from our internal network. Finally, the follow up and conversion need to planned.

**contigency plan must be executed on day 1** from the start of runway.

## Internal Networking

Internal networking objectives is to gather as much as possible information about current on going leads and sales pipeline landscape (ongoing business development) from our known network. Unfortunately, we can only ask such questions with someone that we know or have been worked together on the pasts.

1. notify HR that we will be rolled on in the next two months
2. identify at least 15 leadership that we've worked before across all practices and geography (cross border)
3. start conversation with them (leadership) and capture the leads / active business development
4. list down and track all the leads and BD opportunity 
5. analyze the lists, find the materialization likely hood (% of such opportunities can be assigned to you)

## Follow up & Conversion

Once the leads and on going BD within those two months are gathered, we must poractively follow up and pitch ourselves to the project leads/business development leads. At this rate, our experience/resumes/CV will play a great role. Treat it as job interview. 

There is only three things to do at this stage: 

1. follow up the leads and opportunities
2. ask the leadership to refer youself to their network (worth to try, they might willingly give a referral if we have good bonds with them)
3. keep grinding (repeat the internal networking with other leadership)

## The Contigency

Contigency plan is different with desperate move. Contigency plan must be planned and structured carefully across various aspects including career and financial. Each person contigency plan will be different as everyone has different situation.

Contigency plan to save career.

1. Update linkedin with our latest experience from the firm and let the market know by turning on open to work (only for recruitter).
2. Start look for any open job vacancy on the market from the day one of the two month runway and actively applying.
3. Start to plan for 2nd passive income (its sound too late, but when human is pressured with an unpleasant condition, it will be good boosts). example for software enginer: start writing and recording a course. target to completely it on the 2nd month and sell it immediately after the completion.
4. Start looking for certifications or scholarship (depending on individual preferences) especially during the bench time (if we failed land a role after the roll off date)

Note: doing certifications wont guarantee anything, but at least we elevate our worth.

Contigency plan to save financial condition

1. before new project is confirmed, do not make any big spending. focus on saving.
2. safe investment (deposit, etc)
3. research for the termination terms. Most of big consulting firm will have severance package.

# Going Forward

Hopefully those two months runway will ended up with new project assignment. Going forward, despite the project fastpace with constant challenge, as non managerial consultant, we should prioritize ourself first. Goodluck for all of us.
