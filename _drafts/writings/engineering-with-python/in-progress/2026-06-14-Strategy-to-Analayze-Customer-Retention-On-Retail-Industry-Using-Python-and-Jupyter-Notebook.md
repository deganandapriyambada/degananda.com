# The Importance of Customer Retention on Retail Industry

Customer retention is one the most important metrics in almost every industry (including retail) because it can maximize the company revenue. Long term customer might turn into brand advocates. They will spread good words (word of mouth) about the product or services prodiveded by the organization/company to their friends or family. Regular customer can only turned into brand advocates is they are extremely satisfied with the provided goods or services.

	Funfact: On most retail cases, pareto principle is applied where 20% of loyal customer make up 80% of the revenue.

In terms of customer acquisition cost, retaining customer in retail is cheaper than acquiring new customer because to invite new customer to the retail store, company need to spend marketing cost in form of ads, discount or even KOL (key opinion leader) endorsement. In comparison, loyal customer will still come and spend on the retail store regardless the marketing compaign as they already trust and comfortable with the store.

Identifying loyal customer is as important as providing special treatment for them. Determining factor of loyality need to be formulized from different angle or POV (point of view). Loyal customer can't be defined just based on one solely factor such as number of spending.
