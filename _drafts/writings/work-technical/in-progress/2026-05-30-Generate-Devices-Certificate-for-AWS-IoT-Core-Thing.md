login to the aws console using following links

	https://signin.aws.amazon.com/

navigate to the iot core menu and choose thing management 

select the target thing that need the certificate

Chooese certificate tab under the thing details boxes

Click on the "create certificate button"

activate the certificate

once the certificate is activated, click download all button under the pop up to download all the needed certificates.

there will be total of 5 certificates.

once all the certificate is downloaded, click the "done" button. Note that after the pop up is closed, the certificate can't be redownloaded. It is intended safety mechanism to prevent certificate stealing.

last step is to ensure the certificate is activated, go back to the certificates tab which located below the things details and check if the recently generated certificate status. It should be shown as activated.
